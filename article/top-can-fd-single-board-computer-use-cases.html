Top CAN-FD Single Board Computer Use Cases

Single Board Computers (SBCs) equipped with integrated CAN-FD (Controller Area Network Flexible Data-Rate) transceivers provide edge computing power paired with high-speed, deterministic automotive and industrial networking. By supporting data rates up to 8 Mbps and larger payload capacities than legacy CAN, these SBCs eliminate the need for external expansion hats or adapters. This article outlines the primary use cases where SBCs featuring native CAN-FD transceivers deliver maximum performance and reliability.

1. Advanced Automotive Diagnostics and Telematics

Modern vehicles utilize CAN-FD to handle the increasing volume of sensor data and firmware updates. An SBC with an integrated CAN-FD transceiver acts as a central telemetry gateway or diagnostic bridge. It can capture high-bandwidth bus traffic, log driving events in real time, analyze diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs), and securely relay critical data to cloud platforms via cellular or Wi-Fi connectivity.

2. Electric Vehicle (EV) Powertrain and Battery Management

Electric vehicles rely heavily on rapid data exchange between battery management systems (BMS), motor controllers, inverters, and onboard charging systems. An onboard CAN-FD SBC functions effectively as a centralized controller or test bench interface, monitoring cell voltages, thermal profiles, and charging states while processing power distribution algorithms with minimal communication latency.

3. Robotics and Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs)

Modern autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and robotic arms require fast, synchronized communication between multiple motor drivers, LiDAR sensors, and joint actuators. Protocols such as DroneCAN and Cyphal leverage CAN-FD physical layers to run distributed motion control loops. An embedded SBC processes sensor fusion and path planning locally while issuing rapid motion commands across the robust CAN-FD bus.

4. Industrial Automation and Motion Control

Industrial environments are transitioning from classic CAN/DeviceNet to CAN-FD to support complex multi-axis CNC machines, packaging lines, and robotic assembly stations. SBCs with native CAN-FD transceivers serve as localized programmable logic controllers (PLCs) or edge gateways, collecting telemetry from distributed factory nodes without suffering packet collisions or bandwidth throttling.

5. Heavy Equipment and Agricultural Machinery

Agricultural and construction machinery utilizing modernized ISOBUS or J1939 networks require edge computing nodes for precision farming, implement control, and fleet management. An SBC with onboard CAN-FD hardware withstands high-noise electrical environments to process GPS guidance data, steer automation routines, and regulate hydraulic actuators in real time.

6. Aerospace and Marine Subsystem Interfacing

In uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs) and modern marine electronics, CAN-FD provides a lightweight, interference-resistant alternative to traditional cabling. SBCs deploy onboard to manage flight surface actuators, engine telemetry, power distribution networks, and navigational sensor arrays while minimizing payload weight and power consumption.
