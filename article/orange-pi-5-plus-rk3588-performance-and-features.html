Orange Pi 5 Plus RK3588 Performance and Features

The Orange Pi 5 Plus maximizes the potential of the Rockchip RK3588 system-on-chip (SoC) by translating its advanced processing, artificial intelligence, and multimedia engines into a versatile single-board computer. By combining an octa-core CPU architecture, a multi-core GPU, a 6 TOPS Neural Processing Unit (NPU), and high-throughput I/O controllers, the board delivers desktop-grade computing, edge AI acceleration, and 8K video processing in a compact form factor.

Processing Architecture: CPU and Memory Subsystem

The Orange Pi 5 Plus utilizes the RK3588's 8nm big.LITTLE architecture to balance high-performance tasks with power efficiency.

  • Octa-Core CPU: Consists of four high-performance ARM Cortex-A76 cores clocked up to 2.4 GHz for intensive compute tasks, alongside four energy-efficient ARM Cortex-A55 cores clocked up to 1.8 GHz for background processes.
  • LPDDR4/LPDDR4x RAM: Available in 4GB, 8GB, 16GB, and 32GB configurations, interfacing directly with the RK3588’s multi-channel memory controller to provide high memory bandwidth necessary for multitasking, virtualization, and heavy data pipelines.

Edge AI Acceleration with the 6 TOPS NPU

The integrated Neural Processing Unit (NPU) enables on-device machine learning without offloading computational loads to the CPU or GPU.

  • Performance: Delivers up to 6 TOPS (Tera Operations Per Second) of computing power.
  • Format Support: Supports INT4, INT8, INT16, and FP16 mixed-precision operations.
  • Framework Compatibility: Compatible with major deep learning frameworks including TensorFlow, PyTorch, Caffe, and ONNX.
  • Target Applications: Powers real-time computer vision, object detection, facial recognition, and natural language processing at the edge.

Graphics and Display Capabilities

The RK3588’s multimedia pipeline allows the Orange Pi 5 Plus to handle demanding 3D rendering and complex multi-display setups.

  • ARM Mali-G610 MP4 GPU: Supports OpenGL ES 1.1/2.0/3.2, OpenCL 2.2, and Vulkan 1.1/1.2, providing the power needed for UI acceleration, lightweight gaming, and graphical workloads.
  • Multi-Display Output: Features two HDMI 2.1 ports capable of outputting up to 8K at 60Hz, alongside DisplayPort over USB Type-C and MIPI DSI interfaces, supporting up to four simultaneous independent displays.
  • HDMI Input: Includes an HDMI RX 2.0 port that supports video capture up to 4K at 60Hz directly into the SoC for encoding or processing.

Advanced Video Processing Unit (VPU)

The dedicated VPU inside the RK3588 offloads media processing to handle high-resolution video streams smoothly.

  • Hardware Decoding: Decodes up to 8K@60fps for H.265, VP9, AVS2, and AV1 formats, as well as 8K@30fps for H.264.
  • Hardware Encoding: Encodes up to 8K@30fps in H.265 and H.264, facilitating high-resolution video recording, streaming, and NVR (Network Video Recorder) deployments.

High-Speed I/O and Peripheral Expansion

The Orange Pi 5 Plus breaks out the RK3588's extensive PCIe, USB, and network interfaces into standard physical connectors:

  • Dual 2.5GbE LAN: Utilizes the SoC’s high-bandwidth capabilities to drive two 2.5 Gigabit Ethernet ports for fast networking and routing applications.
  • PCIe 3.0 M.2 Storage: Features an M.2 M-Key slot with PCIe 3.0 x4 lanes for high-speed NVMe SSD integration.
  • Wireless Expansion: Provides an M.2 E-Key slot with PCIe 2.0 and USB interfaces for Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth modules.
  • USB and GPIO: Delivers USB 3.0 Type-A and Type-C ports alongside a 40-pin GPIO header for interfacing with external sensors, industrial equipment, and custom electronics.
