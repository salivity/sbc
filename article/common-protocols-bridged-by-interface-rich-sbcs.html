Common Protocols Bridged by Interface-Rich SBCs

Interface-rich single board computers (SBCs) frequently serve as multi-protocol gateways, translating data streams between isolated hardware layers, legacy systems, and modern networks. This article outlines the most common communication protocols bridged by interface-rich SBCs, examining how these embedded platforms convert low-level, industrial, automotive, and wireless standards into modern, network-accessible data.

1. Modbus RTU (RS-485) to Modbus TCP or MQTT

Industrial automation systems heavily rely on RS-485 serial communication running the Modbus RTU protocol. Interface-rich SBCs bridge these legacy field devices (such as PLCs, power meters, and environmental sensors) to modern IP-based infrastructures.

  • The Bridge: Translates serial Modbus RTU frames into Modbus TCP packets over Ethernet or encapsulates payload data into lightweight MQTT topics for cloud analytics.
  • Primary Use Case: Connecting legacy factory-floor machinery to modern SCADA systems and Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) platforms.

2. CAN Bus to Ethernet and IP Networks

Controller Area Network (CAN) bus is the standard for robust, real-time communication in automotive, aerospace, and robotics applications. SBCs equipped with native CAN controllers or SPI-based CAN transceivers act as diagnostic and telemetry bridges.

  • The Bridge: Translates raw CAN frames (including CANopen and J1939 protocols) into UDP/TCP packets, WebSockets, or HTTP REST endpoints over Wi-Fi or Ethernet.
  • Primary Use Case: Real-time vehicle telemetry, robotic fleet monitoring, and remote diagnostics.

3. Low-Level Bus Protocols (I2C, SPI, UART) to IP-Based APIs

Microcontrollers, raw sensors, and edge peripherals communicate using bare-metal synchronous and asynchronous serial interfaces. SBCs provide onboard GPIO, hardware UART, I2C buses, and SPI channels to aggregate this low-level data.

  • The Bridge: Collects raw data from I2C/SPI sensors or UART-connected microcontrollers, processes the data, and exposes it over standard web protocols (HTTP, WebSockets, gRPC) or publishes it via MQTT.
  • Primary Use Case: Sensor hubs, edge data processing, and rapid hardware-in-the-loop prototyping.

4. Wireless IoT (Zigbee, BLE, LoRaWAN) to Local Area Networks

Many environmental and edge sensors operate over low-power wireless standards that cannot directly connect to standard IP networks. Interface-rich SBCs equipped with onboard radios, HATs, or USB dongles serve as central base stations.

  • The Bridge: Bridges short-range wireless protocols (Bluetooth Low Energy, Zigbee, Z-Wave) or long-range low-bandwidth protocols (LoRaWAN) to Ethernet, Wi-Fi, or cellular (LTE-M/NB-IoT) networks.
  • Primary Use Case: Smart building management, agriculture monitoring, and automated asset tracking.

5. Legacy RS-232 Serial to Telnet or Secure Shell (SSH)

Legacy laboratory equipment, network switches, and medical hardware often feature only an RS-232 serial interface for configuration and data export.

  • The Bridge: Maps RS-232 serial streams to virtual COM ports, raw TCP sockets, or secure web consoles over Ethernet.
  • Primary Use Case: Remote console management, server rack administration, and extending the operational lifespan of legacy infrastructure.
