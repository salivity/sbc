Rock 5 Model A Performance in a Compact Footprint

The Radxa Rock 5 Model A (Rock 5A) achieves high-end processing performance within a credit-card-sized form factor by integrating the octa-core Rockchip RK3588S system-on-chip (SoC), an advanced 6 TOPS Neural Processing Unit (NPU), and high-bandwidth LPDDR4x memory. By utilizing an optimized 8nm silicon manufacturing process, a streamlined package layout, and dedicated hardware accelerators, the board provides desktop-grade compute, multimedia, and machine learning capabilities without exceeding standard single-board computer (SBC) dimensions.

High-Density Octa-Core Processing

At the core of the Rock 5A's performance density is the Rockchip RK3588S processor. The SoC utilizes an Arm DynamIQ big.LITTLE configuration consisting of four high-performance Cortex-A76 cores clocked up to 2.4 GHz and four power-efficient Cortex-A55 cores clocked at 1.8 GHz. The underlying 8nm lithography allows for high transistor density, maximizing clock speeds and instructions-per-clock (IPC) efficiency while maintaining a minimal silicon footprint and low power consumption.

The RK3588S Space-Saving Package

The standard RK3588 SoC used in larger boards features extensive I/O routing that requires a substantial physical footprint. The Rock 5A adopts the "S" variant (RK3588S), which trims redundant lanes and packages the core architecture into a more compact footprint. This allows Radxa to fit flagship silicon onto an 85 x 56 mm board layout—the standard Raspberry Pi form factor—without sacrificing primary CPU, GPU, or AI computing performance.

Hardware Offloading via Integrated GPU and NPU

To prevent the central processor from bottlenecking during intensive tasks, the Rock 5A routes specific workloads to dedicated hardware blocks:

  • Graphics and Media Processing: The integrated Arm Mali-G610 MP4 GPU handles complex 3D rendering and graphical interfaces, supporting OpenCL 2.2, OpenGL ES 3.2, and Vulkan 1.2.
  • AI Acceleration: A built-in NPU delivers up to 6 TOPS of INT8 compute power, processing artificial intelligence and computer vision models directly on edge devices with minimal latency.
  • Hardware Video Codecs: Dedicated encoding and decoding engines support up to 8K resolution video decoding at 60 frames per second and 8K encoding at 30 frames per second for modern codecs, including H.265, AV1, and VP9.

High-Bandwidth Memory Architecture

Processing cores require high data throughput to maintain peak performance. The Rock 5A implements up to 16GB of LPDDR4x memory operating over a 64-bit wide memory bus with speeds up to 4224 MT/s. The physical placement of the low-profile LPDDR4x chips directly adjacent to the SoC minimizes trace lengths, ensuring signal integrity and low-latency access essential for real-time edge computing.

Efficient Power Delivery and Thermal Layout

Sustaining high performance in a small footprint requires strict thermal and electrical management. The Rock 5A uses an advanced Power Management Integrated Circuit (PMIC) and flexible USB Type-C Power Delivery (PD) to efficiently distribute power across performance states. Component placement on both sides of the printed circuit board leaves the top surface clear for standard heatsinks and active cooling solutions, allowing the board to avoid thermal throttling during continuous compute loads.
